# Mobile Construction Vehicle generation prompts

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production directional source sheet for an optional HD RTS Mobile
Construction Vehicle

Primary request: Create exactly five isolated full-vehicle views of the same
original heavy six-wheel deployable construction truck in a strict horizontal
5-column by 1-row sheet. Direction order is N, NE, E, SE and S. In each cell,
rotate the actual vehicle about its vertical world axis while the orthographic
65-degree overhead RTS camera, scale, proportions and upper-left lighting stay
fixed. The vehicle has a folded construction crane, rear machinery modules,
stowed stabilizer legs and a compact armored cab, visually related to the
matching faction Construction Yard while remaining a drivable vehicle.

Shared style: polished hand-painted late-20th-century RTS production sprite,
original generic military construction machinery rather than a copy of an
existing game asset. Crisp realistic detail and strongly readable silhouette
at small size. Transparent or removable uniform dark background, no scenery.
Retain one restrained soft contact shadow directly below each vehicle.

GDI variant: weathered warm-gray/olive machinery, pale concrete-colored body
panels, golden-yellow crane and safety details, dark rugged tires.

Nod variant: charcoal and black machinery, deep-red armor/crane accents,
restrained red utility lights, dark rugged tires.

Constraints: exactly five views, one vehicle per equal cell, whole vehicle and
stabilizers visible, identical vehicle design and size across facings, no
deployed building, turret, weapon, people, text, letters, faction logos,
insignia, watermark, terrain, dust, smoke, selection ring, health bar or frame
dividers. No copyrighted sprite or packaging artwork.
