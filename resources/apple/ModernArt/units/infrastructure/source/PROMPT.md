# Infrastructure generation prompts

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: production source sheet for optional high-resolution classic RTS
building replacements

Shared composition: Create exactly three isolated, complete buildings in one
strict horizontal 3-column by 1-row sheet. Left is a compact two-stack Power
Plant, centre is the faction's infantry barracks, and right is a wide deployed
Construction Yard with crane and vehicle assembly bay. Use one fixed
orthographic 65-degree overhead RTS camera, matching scale, ground plane and
upper-left lighting for every object. Keep each building wholly inside its
equal cell with a readable silhouette and generous separation.

Shared style: polished hand-painted late-20th-century RTS production art,
original generic industrial/military architecture rather than a copy of an
existing game sprite. Crisp realistic detail that remains legible at small
size. Transparent or removable uniform dark background, no scenery. Retain a
subtle soft contact shadow directly beneath each building.

GDI variant: warm concrete, weathered steel and restrained golden-yellow
equipment panels. The barracks is a low fortified military building with an
open drill/assembly deck. The Construction Yard is a broad practical vehicle
bay with a yellow overhead crane. Optimistic utilitarian design.

Nod variant: charcoal steel, dark concrete and restrained deep-red armor and
light panels. The barracks is a tall angular fortified infantry facility with
a dramatic central tower but no symbol or copied sculpture. The Construction
Yard is a broad covert industrial vehicle bay with a dark red crane. Severe,
asymmetric high-tech design.

Constraints: exactly three buildings and no loose vehicles or people; no
overlap between cells; no text, letters, faction marks, logos, insignia,
watermark, nuclear/radiation symbol, smoke, fire, projectile, selection ring,
health bar, terrain or decorative border. Do not reproduce any copyrighted
game sprite or packaging artwork.
