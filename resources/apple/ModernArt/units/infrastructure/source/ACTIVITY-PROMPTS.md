# Infrastructure activity generation prompts

The built-in image-generation tool edited the existing faction infrastructure
atlases as exact visual references. All four requests used the
`precise-object-edit` use case, fixed isometric camera, identical scale,
footprint, anchor, lighting and materials across five horizontal keyframes,
with no units, text, scenery, effects or borders.

## GDI Construction Yard

Create five transparent frames of the referenced GDI Construction Yard during
production. Move only the gold gantry crane and its hook/cable: left and raised,
toward centre and descending, centred and low, toward right and rising, right
and raised. Small amber work lights may alternate.

## Nod Construction Yard

Create the same five transparent production phases for the referenced Nod yard,
preserving dark steel/deep-red materials and using restrained red work lights.

## GDI Barracks

Create five transparent activation phases of the referenced GDI Barracks:
front door closed, quarter-open, half-open with warm interior light, fully open,
and closing halfway. Change only the door/interior and small status lights.

## Nod Hand

Create five transparent activation phases of the referenced Nod infantry
building: sealed entrance, energised red seams, half-open, fully active with a
restrained red interior, and closing halfway. Preserve the tower and all other
geometry exactly.
